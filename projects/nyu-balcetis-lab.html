Social Perception and Motivation Lab

Dr. Balcetis · NYU Department of Psychology · New York, NY · May 2026 – August 2026

The SPAM Lab studies motivation, goal pursuit, and social perception. I joined as the lab launched a new research programme investigating how junk-food companies use social media marketing to shape adolescents' food preferences and eating behaviors.

Eye-Tracking Study — Food Advertising & Visual Attention

Primary research assistant for the lab’s eye-tracking studies
  • Ran an eye-tracking study of how adolescents view Instagram food adverts, and how racial representation shapes engagement.
  • Secured Meta's approval for the lab's social-media advertising study, clearing the way for the platform data the research design depends on.
  • Partnered directly with Google on an eye-tracking study led by a former SPAM Lab member now at Google, recruiting and running 40 participants in a single week and analyzing the eye-tracking data. Participants judged whether handwriting samples matched, with particular letters either highlighted or left unmarked.
  • Ran lab meetings in Dr. Balcetis's absence and organized whole-lab data analysis sessions.

NYU CCL Psychology Workshop (High School Outreach)

Led workshop · Summer 2026

Led an outreach program for under-resourced middle and high school students in New York, built around six modules — Visual Illusions, Forensic Psychology, Personality, Neuroscience, Developmental Psychology, and Careers — using Malcolm Gladwell's Blink as a storytelling and engagement model.

  • Designed large portions of the curriculum and presentations, delivered as two-hour interactive sessions to groups of 20–40 students and at one event for over 200 students at NYU's Kimmel Center.
  • Led team meetings with Dr. Balcetis's lab, driving group decisions and module ownership.
  • Delivered workshops on targeted fast-food advertising as part of a longitudinal study, measuring students' snack and drink choices and willingness to pay before and after each session.
