QOVES — Research Intern

London, England · Full-time, in-office · July 2026 – September 2026

QOVES is a consumer AI facial-analysis company that grounds its facial aesthetics, biometric, and cosmetic-trend products in peer-reviewed research. I worked under Research Analyst Santiago Grandas Forero and Chief Scientist Macken Murphy on a mix of data analysis, product research, and academic literature synthesis — sitting at the intersection of facial aesthetics, biometric data, and consumer behavior.

Automated 10,000-City Environmental Database

Built pipeline · AI agents & Google APIs

Built the automated data pipeline behind a 10,000-city environmental database, so that when a customer selects the city they live in, their skincare protocol and product recommendations adjust automatically to local conditions such as humidity and air salinity. Each city draws on dozens of variables from primary sources, which I collected and kept updated with AI agents and Google APIs, so the system runs without manual input after my internship ended.

Global Cosmetic Surgery & Beauty-Spending Landscape

Led project · Cross-country data synthesis

Led a research deep dive into cosmetic surgery and beauty-spending patterns across roughly 40 countries — compiling, cleaning, and structuring data into a unified cross-country dataset, then building the visualizations used across the team to communicate the findings.

Facial Morphology & Perception Literature Reviews

Literature synthesis · Biometric data
  • Synthesized peer-reviewed anthropological and biometric literature on how nose morphology varies with geographic origin, to ground one of QOVES's facial-analysis metrics.
  • Reviewed the literature on perceived attractiveness of freckles across demographic groups, combining published findings with QOVES's own biometric data.

MLLM vs. Human Attractiveness Ratings (Grandas et al., 2026)

Research assistant · Preprint support

Supported Santiago Grandas Forero's preprint manuscript comparing how multimodal large language models rate facial attractiveness against human raters — handling verification passes and reviewer-comment responses. The preprint found that multimodal LLMs systematically overestimate attractiveness relative to human raters.

Customer Behavioral Dataset Analysis

Applied data analysis

Analyzed a large customer dataset (60,000+ entries) to explore behavioral and appearance-related well-being patterns among QOVES users. The emphasis throughout was methodological rigor — careful handling of missing data, choosing appropriate denominators, and reporting with confidence intervals rather than point estimates alone.
