Independent Project — Subconscious Priming & Athletic Performance

Led project · Mentored by Dr. Ratner · $750 URCA grant under review

Originated as my Extended Project Qualification (national distinction, maximum grade), and now being developed into a mentored follow-up study with Dr. Ratner, with a $750 UCSB URCA research grant currently under review to support it.

  • Focus: Whether speed-related subconscious primes increase sprint performance relative to neutral, slow, or no-prime conditions.
  • My contribution: End-to-end ownership — protocol and materials design, data plan, ethics considerations, and analysis plan.
  • Methods: Timed sprints under controlled prime conditions, awareness checks, fatigue ratings.
  • Status: Original study completed; extended analysis and a funded follow-up study in development.
