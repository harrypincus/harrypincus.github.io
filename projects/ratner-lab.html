Independent Project — Subconscious Priming & Athletic Performance

Led project · Mentored by Dr. Ratner · IRB-approved

Originated as my Extended Project Qualification (national distinction), a double-blind priming study I designed and ran at 16. As a freshman I brought it to Dr. Ratner, and together we adapted it for his lab and secured IRB approval to run it.

  • Focus: Whether speed-related subconscious primes increase sprint performance relative to neutral, slow, or no-prime conditions.
  • My contribution: End-to-end ownership — protocol and materials design, data plan, ethics application, and analysis plan.
  • Methods: Double-blind design with counterbalanced prime conditions, timed sprints clocked by three independent timers, awareness checks, and fatigue ratings.
  • Status: Original study completed; the lab version was approved but shelved.
